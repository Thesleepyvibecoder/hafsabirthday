# Version 15 Notes

## Reasons-page music
- The existing `assets/hafsa-birthday-song.mp3` is now used only on `reasons.html`.
- The music control remains on the Reasons page and starts/pauses the song on click.
- Added a fixed top-of-page hint: `Tap here to make the experience better` with a directional arrow pointing toward the music button.
- The hint disappears once the user scrolls down more than 24px and reappears when they return to the top.
- The hint is responsive for mobile widths.
- Removed the V14 music button/audio from the other pages so this song is exclusive to the Reasons section for now. Future sections can receive their own songs/voice notes.

All existing V14 functionality is otherwise preserved.
