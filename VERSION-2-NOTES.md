# Birthday Website V2

This version keeps the original site's navigation, rounded buttons, fonts, and overall interaction language, while adding a new birthday-entry experience.

## Entry flow
- Locked until 16 September 2026 at 12:00 AM Karachi time (PKT, UTC+5).
- Before unlock: only the countdown screen is visible.
- At/after unlock: the countdown is replaced by the birthday reveal.
- Late opening: the reveal still appears immediately, with a different line acknowledging that midnight already passed.
- Candle click/keyboard interaction extinguishes the flame and enables the Enter button.
- Enter goes to the existing Cake page.

## Important
The biryani cake is intentionally a first-pass CSS concept. We can replace it with a more polished custom illustration/image once the visual direction is approved.
